Week 1 - Self Introduction

Introduced myself confidently in front of the class. Shared my educational background, interests, career goals, hobbies, strengths and personal values. This activity improved confidence and public speaking ability.

Week 2 - Communication Basics

Learned fundamentals of communication including verbal communication, non-verbal communication, listening skills, eye contact and tone control. Understood how communication helps in academics and career.

Week 3 - Speaking Practice

Performed speaking tasks to remove hesitation and improve fluency. Worked on pronunciation, sentence framing and confidence while talking.

Week 4 - Personality Development

Focused on grooming, discipline, positive thinking, confidence and attitude. Learned how personality impacts success in interviews and professional life.

Week 5 - JAM Session

Participated in Just A Minute speaking session. Spoke continuously for one minute on random topics. Improved spontaneous speaking and vocabulary.

Week 6 - Extempore

Delivered speeches on instant topics without preparation. This improved thinking speed, presentation skill and stage confidence.

Week 7 - Resume Writing

Learned how to create professional resume. Added education, technical skills, projects, achievements and certifications.

Week 8 - Resume Improvement

Focused on ATS format, professional keywords, layout improvement, strong summary and clean formatting.

Week 9 - Group Discussion

Learned rules of GD, teamwork, active listening and expressing views politely.

Week 10 - Current Affairs Discussion

Participated in discussions on technology, economy and social topics. Built awareness and logical thinking.

Week 11 - Leadership Skills

Learned how to lead a team, manage conflicts and motivate members.

Week 12 - Confidence Building

Regular speaking practice helped in reducing fear and improving stage presence.

Week 13 - Advanced GD Round

Handled challenging topics with logical arguments and better communication.

Week 14 - Final Reflection

Overall PESE classes improved my confidence, communication, resume making, teamwork and personality growth.
